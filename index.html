Wreath math

The craft stall sells greenery by the bundle, the bow eats ribbon faster than anyone admits, and the wreath at the fair has a price nobody can justify. Wreath math turns the frame's diameter into bundles of greenery, the bow's loops into meters of ribbon to buy, and the whole materials list into a cost and a margin per wreath. Exact arithmetic, every borrowed number labeled.

Open the calculators

The three tools

  • Frame plan - the frame's diameter becomes its circumference and the bundles of greenery to cover it.
  • Ribbon plan - bow loops and tails become centimeters of ribbon and the meters to buy.
  • Cost plan - frame, greenery and ribbon become the cost to make and the margin at your selling price.

Labeled norms

Labeled circumference: 3.1416 times the diameter. Labeled coverage: one bundle of greenery covers about 25 cm of frame - illustrative, since bundle density and how full you like it move the number. Prices are your own inputs; the craft stall's tags win.
